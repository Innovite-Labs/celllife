CellLife — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026 · Developed by Innovite Labs

CellLife collects no data. It requests no internet permission and is technically incapable of transmitting anything off your device.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device data. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs of any kind in this app.

What stays on your device

  • Your temperature thresholds, chosen alert sounds, and display preferences are saved only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Battery readings (temperature, current, voltage, charge level, health, cycle count) are read from the Android system, shown to you live, and never recorded, uploaded, or retained after the app closes.

Uninstalling CellLife removes all of it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Notifications — to show battery status and temperature threshold alerts.
  • Foreground service — to keep watching battery temperature while the app is closed, so alerts still fire.
  • Vibrate — optional vibration alongside an alert.
  • Run at startup — to resume monitoring after you restart your phone, if you had it enabled.

CellLife does not request internet, location, contacts, storage, camera, or microphone access.

Children

CellLife is a device utility and is not directed at children.
